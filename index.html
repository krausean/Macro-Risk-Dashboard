Don't let a defensive plan become a permanent exit

If you deliberately raise cash during deterioration, decide in advance how it gets redeployed. A simple personal framework is to divide the reserve into four equal tranches and begin redeploying as the composite score falls materially from its peak and labour/lending signals stabilize.

How to read this dashboard 2-minute guide
1. Start with the regime

Calm, Watch, Elevated or High tells you whether several indicators are deteriorating together. It is not a recession probability.

2. Look at the score drivers

Each card now shows how many points it contributes. Canada-only cards are context and do not change the U.S.-led recession score.

3. Look for confirmation

One warning is not enough. Jobs + lending + rates deteriorating together is more meaningful than any single number.

4. Use the cash band as a review prompt

The band is your pre-set rule, not an automatic sell instruction. Re-entry planning matters just as much as getting defensive.

PRE-COMMITTED RE-ENTRY PLAN Defensive market timing often fails because investors sell during deterioration but are too afraid to buy back during the panic. A re-entry plan is meant to prevent that.

Don't let a defensive plan become a permanent exit

If you deliberately raise cash during deterioration, decide in advance how it gets redeployed. A simple personal framework is to divide the reserve into four equal tranches and begin redeploying as the composite score falls materially from its peak and labour/lending signals stabilize. The dashboard does not execute trades.

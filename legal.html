Conservative source design

This personal, non-commercial project intentionally avoids third-party market indices and news aggregation. It does not use the FRED API, ICE/BofA indices, S&P indices, VIX data, Google News, newspaper article text, institutional logos, advertising, analytics, tracking scripts, or external JavaScript libraries.

The composite score and cash bands are calculations made by this site's code. They are not official measures, ratings, forecasts, or endorsements from any source institution.

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics

The site retrieves selected BLS series through the public BLS API and performs its own calculations.

BLS notice: BLS.gov cannot vouch for the data or analyses derived from these data after the data have been retrieved from BLS.gov.

No BLS logo is used.

Bank of Canada

Source: Bank of Canada. Selected interest-rate data are retrieved through the Valet API and transformed/presented by this site. The Bank of Canada does not endorse this application. No Bank of Canada logo or wordmark is used.

U.S. Treasury and Federal Reserve Board

Treasury yield-curve data come from the U.S. Department of the Treasury's official Daily Treasury Rates feed. SLOOS lending-standard data and selected official release links come from the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. The site links to original releases rather than copying article text.

Investment disclaimer

Nothing on this site executes a trade or knows the user's complete financial circumstances. Displayed allocation bands are a personal monitoring rule-set for educational planning. Investment decisions remain with the user.

Source terms and website policies can change. Re-check them before commercializing the project, adding third-party data, advertising, or providing it as a service to other users.
